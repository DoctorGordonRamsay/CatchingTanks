# Catching Tanks

> Asymmetric multiplayer. One side hunts. The other fights back.

---

## Concept

Catching Tanks reframes the hide-and-seek formula as a tactical team game. Defenders aren't waiting to be found — they're outmanoeuvring pursuers, disrupting captures, and pulling teammates out of the enemy's grip. The map offers little cover by design. Survival demands aggression.

---

## Teams

### Grabber Bots — *The Hunt*
Grabbers win by capturing every Tank before the clock runs out. They carry one Tank at a time, which means a single Grabber can never close out a match alone. While holding a target, they can temporarily disable nearby Tanks to fend off rescue attempts — but their real strength is coordination. The team's numerical advantage is the pressure; organised pursuit is the execution.

### Tank Bots — *The Resistance*
Tanks win by keeping at least one member free when time expires. Armed with a turret that breaks a Grabber's grip and briefly stalls them, and slightly faster on their feet, Tanks have the tools to disrupt, dodge, and delay. Crucially, they can rescue captured allies — turning every successful grab into a potential reversal. Passive play is a losing strategy.

---

## Win Conditions

| Team | Victory |
|------|---------|
| Grabbers | All Tanks captured before time expires |
| Tanks | At least one Tank free at full time |
