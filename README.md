# Catching Tanks

> Asymmetric multiplayer.

---
## Recommended Set up

+ Player Limit: 4 - 16
+ Recommended player count: 7+
+ Technical requirements: Only compatible on Windows.
+ Communication: Discord or any other voice chat services with friends.

---

## Concept

Catching Tanks is a hide-and-seek and tag-inspired asymmetric multiplayer game involving Grabbers attempting to catch all Tanks before the time runs out.

---

## Teams

### Grabbers — *The Aggressor Team*
Grabbers win by capturing every Tank before the clock runs out. They carry one Tank at a time, which means that Grabbers have to work together. While holding a target, they can temporarily disable nearby Tanks to fend off rescue attempts — but their real strength is coordination. They have the numbers advantage.

### Tank — *The Defending Team*
Tanks win by keeping at least one member free when time expires. Armed with a laser turret that breaks a Grabber's grip and briefly stalls them, and slightly faster on their feet, Tanks have the tools to disrupt, dodge, and delay. Crucially, they can rescue captured allies, increasing their odds of winning the match.

---

## Win Conditions

| Team | Victory |
|------|---------|
| Grabbers | All Tanks captured before time expires |
| Tanks | At least one Tank free when the timer ends |
